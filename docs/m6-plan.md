# M6 — explicit dated conversion slice

Immutable conversion books contain stable-ID FX and product-specific UOM rows, positive rational factors and explicit half-open dates. Owner submission and independent Approver review are required. Direct-key date overlaps reject within a book and across approved books. There is no inferred inverse, triangulation, deletion or overlapping amendment; future adjacent schedules use new books. Rows and approval/capture revisions remain immutable evidence.

Quote requests opt into a target currency and requested unit. Resolve direct native-currency→target FX and requested-unit→native-product-unit UOM at the pricing date. Identity legs require no rate. Normalize the requested quantity exactly to native thousandths before rule or source eligibility; reject precision loss and bounds violations. Contract bands remain explicitly in native product units. Combine UOM and FX factors and round native list/rule/cost unit amounts once to target cents, half away from zero, then use existing negotiated discount/extension/rebate rules. This unit-first convention is not native-total conversion and does not promise amount invariance across quantity splitting, UOM choices or round trips. Frozen native amounts and quantities remain the reference; never reconstruct them by inverse conversion.

Approved native grid/list/contract sources may feed converted quotes. Their original discounts are already reflected; rebates do not carry forward. Grid/list/contract authoring stays native to prevent conversion chaining. Quote approval cycles freeze exact conversion direction, row, rational, dates, book version, approval/capture revisions, native basis and rounding policy. Existing absent-field serialization and CSV layouts remain unchanged.

Atomic multi-line replacement resolves all replacements and retained lines in one transaction, preserving historical cycles and contributor exclusions. This supports whole-quote currency changes and simultaneous repair of stale sources; partial success is impossible.

Converted invoice analytics explicitly selects one reporting currency and date period. Each recorded invoice's native invoice/cost totals convert once at its original date, then aggregate; approved reversals subtract that exact original converted pair and replacements use their own context. Margin is converted revenue minus converted cost. Native product units/quantities stay visible; analytics does not invent a common UOM or add incompatible quantities. Save immutable report snapshots with source revision, invoice/correction IDs and frozen rates; export stable CSV. Missing rates reject the whole result. Reports are presentation calculations, not postings or quote projections.

Native book/import/review, quote conversion and reporting controls share engine/API commands. CSV imports are atomic and bounded, exports formula-safe/no-overwrite. Validate exact arithmetic, half-open boundaries, source qualification, missing/inverse/double conversion, mixed-currency comparisons, old workspace bytes, failed commits and historic evidence; independent reviews and final-source Windows/macOS/Linux release/process checks remain required.
