# Design direction
Copperline is a provisional industrial pricing workbench, unrelated to any vendor. Dense decision tables and an explained price waterfall are its focus. Quiet pale-blue canvas #F1F5F8, white working paper #FFFFFF, deep maritime #183E54 navigation, copper #B85B35 actions, slate #536575 supporting text, teal #237B72 healthy margin. Use the toolkit's bundled licensed sans serif, proportional numbers in labels and tabular alignment in tables. No vendor fonts or copied art.
Layout: narrow left workspace rail; top customer/date context; main live price table and compact right quote/waterfall inspector; functional drill-down views for rules, frozen lists, approvals and audit. Left aligned labels, right aligned money. One visual emphasis: a stacked invoice/rebate/cost/margin waterfall with direct amounts. Avoid marketing decoration and tiled KPI cards. Tables keep context across tabs. Role selector says local demo identity; error/status strip gives specific corrective action.
Review: native desktop density suits a pricing operator better than a landing-page treatment. Copper is limited to actions; status has text as well as color. Every business button calls the engine command boundary. No animations or network assets.
