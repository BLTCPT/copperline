# Gaps

- Full functional parity is a long-term target. Current milestone is synthetic local pricing/quote workflow; it is not a Pricefx replacement or compatible API.
- No enterprise identity, multi-tenancy, authenticated multi-user permissions, delegated approval hierarchy, encryption or signed artifacts.
- No predictive optimization, elasticity/demand estimation, AI recommendations, enterprise analytics warehouse, competitor data, CPQ configuration/BOM, tax/freight engine, external settlement/payment reconciliation or ERP adapters.
- Explicit dated direct FX/product-UOM conversion is implemented for quotes and FX reporting. No inferred inverse/multihop, approved overlapping amendments, live rate feed, arbitrary monetary precision or UOM aggregation in analytics. Source authoring stays native; catalog editor still defaults to USD.
- Converted quote-to-actual full booking is implemented with immutable booked/native evidence. Partial booking, partial line credits, fulfillment/delivery tracking and customer invoice documents remain missing. Booked rows permit full reversal only; replacement requires a distinct approved quote and new booking. Ordinary external invoice add/import remains catalog-native.
- Snapshot history uses full state per revision and scans on open. Portable complete-history backup and guarded restore into a new directory are implemented, including pending/recovery evidence. Measured synthetic workloads cover 1,000 invoices/20 quotes/327 snapshots, not enterprise volume. Database migration, retention, incremental backups and resumable restore remain gaps; see performance.md and backup.md.
- Live grid has candidate/last-approved lifecycle and lists have whole-document approval. Quotes may explicitly select active grid or published list prices with legacy exact-date/minimum-quantity qualification, or approved customer contract editions with date periods and quantity bands. Multi-customer contracts, converted source authoring and formula stacking remain gaps; nothing writes external systems.
- Rebate agreements support approved retrospective tiers and frozen accruals. Full reversals/atomic replacements and signed local settlement recognition exist. Partial credit allocation, amendments, renewals, progressive tiers, customer credit-note delivery, accounting-period close, external settlement reconciliation and payment posting remain missing.
- Rules are single-winner. No arbitrary scripting, stacked promotions, formula editor or delegated/general approval routing. Quotes support frozen commercial → conditional Finance review.
- Windows release compilation, all six process workflows, the small synthetic workload/archive round trip and portable packaging passed M7 CI. Interactive Windows desktop behavior remains unverified. UI accessibility and small-screen coverage need deeper testing.
