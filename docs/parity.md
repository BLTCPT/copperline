# Functional capability map

Reviewed 2026-10-13 against tested M9 runtime source `a36d4877228b14732aff84d77d32776e9c8260ae` and evidence commit `64dd073`. Copperline is an independent, synthetic local pricing workbench. This compares workflows described in official public Pricefx materials with concrete Copperline behavior; it is not an exhaustive inventory, API compatibility claim, replacement claim or parity percentage. Vendor descriptions are not independently verified performance results. No authenticated materials, vendor implementation or artwork inform this comparison.

**Implemented/tested subset** means the narrowly named workflow exists and has executable evidence. **Partial** means some of the broader capability works but substantial parts are absent. **Missing** means no working equivalent exists. The statuses apply only within the stated scope; local demo role checks do not establish enterprise authorization.

## Pricing and commercial workflows

| Capability and public reference | Copperline status | Tested behavior and remaining boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Catalog, cost inputs and rules — [Price Management](https://www.pricefx.com/platform/price-management/) describes centralized prices and configurable pricing rules | Implemented/tested subset | Product/customer identities, effective-dated versioned costs and single-winner fixed-price, list-discount or cost-markup rules; deterministic precedence and missing-cost errors. No formula language, index feeds or arbitrary stacked conditions. [Golden/precedence/invariant tests](../crates/pricing/src/lib.rs), [CSV and atomicity tests](../crates/engine/tests/commercial_review.rs). |
| Price-list calculation and review — public [Price Lists](https://knowledge.pricefx.com/configuration-engineer-knowledge-base/guides/price-setting-concepts/price-lists) describes calculated documents and approval states | Implemented/tested subset | Frozen whole-list calculation, submit/review and separate local publication. Publication sends nothing externally. [List regressions](../crates/engine/tests/review_regressions.rs), [process flow](../scripts/e2e.py). |
| Active grid pricing — public [Live Price Grids](https://knowledge.pricefx.com/pricefx-unity-documentation/pricefx-capabilities/price-setting/live-price-grids) describes recalculable rows and active prices | Implemented/tested subset | Separate candidate and last-approved item, independent review, archived approvals; recalculation or rejection preserves the active price. No scheduled recalculation, auto-approval or large-grid scale claim. [Grid lifecycle tests](../crates/engine/tests/commercial_review.rs). |
| Approved-price selection in quotes | Implemented/tested subset | Explicit current approved grid or published list selection with exact point-date/context and quantity-floor checks; optional customer-contract resolver. Source evidence freezes through later changes. No implicit grid/list lookup or recursive source discounting. [Source tests](../crates/engine/tests/pricing_sources.rs), [selection contract](pricing-sources.md). |
| Quote calculation and explanation — [Quoting](https://www.pricefx.com/platform/quoting/) describes transparent price components and margin guardrails | Implemented/tested subset | Flat quote lines, exact unit/extension/rebate rounding, explained waterfall, hard/soft guardrails and atomic draft line replacement. Projected rebates are not invoice postings. [Pricing tests](../crates/pricing/src/lib.rs), [replacement tests](../crates/engine/tests/quote_replace_m5.rs), [process flow](../scripts/e2e.py). |
| Configurable quote approvals — [Quoting](https://www.pricefx.com/platform/quoting/) describes routing and automatic approval | Partial | Owner-only changes, frozen commercial → conditional Finance stages, currency/discount/margin thresholds, contributor exclusions and distinct reviewers. Every quote requires review; no auto-approval, delegation, parallel routing or general organization hierarchy. [Approval regressions](../crates/engine/tests/approval_m3_review.rs), [governance](quote-governance.md). |
| Customer agreements — [Agreements](https://www.pricefx.com/solutions/agreements/) describes formula/index-driven contracts, recalculation and renewal analysis | Partial | Immutable approved customer/currency editions with half-open dates, quantity bands, priority/conflict rules, fixed prices or frozen approved origins. Latest started edition cannot resurrect an expired predecessor. No formula/index clauses, automatic renewals, multi-customer contracts or converted source authoring. [Adversarial tests](../crates/engine/tests/contracts_m5_review.rs), [process flow](../scripts/contracts_e2e.py). |
| Explicit FX and product-UOM conversion | Implemented/tested subset | Independently approved direct rational rate books; exact native quantity eligibility, combined unit rounding, frozen quote/report evidence. USD/EUR/GBP only; no inferred inverse/multihop, overlapping approved amendments or live rate feed. [Financial tests](../crates/engine/tests/conversion_m6_financial.rs), [process flow](../scripts/conversions_e2e.py), [rounding contract](conversions.md). |
| Approved quote → recorded actual | Implemented/tested subset | Atomic full-quote booking, once per quote ID lifetime; immutable recorded amounts, approval/contributor evidence and non-posted native basis. No partial booking, shipment, customer invoice issuance or ERP posting. [Booking tests](../crates/engine/tests/bookings_m8.rs), [process flow](../scripts/bookings_e2e.py). |
| Correctable actuals and quantity credits | Implemented/tested subset | Independent Finance-approved full reversals; raw invoices may have atomic replacement. Mutually exclusive cumulative quantity credits exhaust original cents and preserve original FX/native evidence. Booked replacements require a distinct approved quote. No value-only credits, credit amendment/reversal or credit-note delivery. [Correction tests](../crates/engine/tests/corrections_m4.rs), [credit invariants](../crates/engine/tests/m9_credits.rs). |
| Local invoice-period control | Implemented/tested subset | Finance-reviewed half-open period in one recorded currency freezes exact gross/reversed/credited/net invoice basis and blocks affected later changes. Reporting FX and rebates remain outside the close. No accounting compliance, reopen or next-period carry-forward. [Period tests](../crates/engine/tests/m9_periods.rs), [credit/close process flow](../scripts/credits_e2e.py). |
| Rebates and settlement — [Rebates](https://www.pricefx.com/platform/rebates) describes multiple program structures, accruals, forecasting and payouts | Partial | Approved retrospective revenue tiers, original-period correction/credit eligibility, frozen alternative full-period accruals and additive signed local settlement adjustments with exact baseline checks. No progressive/growth programs, forecasting, payments or external accounting reconciliation. [Commercial tests](../crates/engine/tests/commercial_review.rs), [settlement review](../crates/engine/tests/settlement_m4_review.rs), [process flow](../scripts/reconciliation_e2e.py). |
| Scenario comparison — [Price Management](https://www.pricefx.com/platform/price-management/) describes price simulation | Implemented/tested subset | Compare deterministic evaluations for the same product/customer and common output currency. No demand, volume-response or causal profit estimate. [Scenario/currency tests](../crates/engine/tests/conversion_m6_financial.rs). |
| Analytics, segmentation and alerts — public [Analytics rollups](https://knowledge.pricefx.com/pricefx-unity-documentation/pricefx-capabilities/analytics/rollups) describes multidimensional aggregation and comparisons | Partial | Recorded customer/currency invoice margin, gross/reversed/credited/net totals and immutable explicit reporting-currency snapshots. Active counts exclude fully reversed or fully quantity-credited originals. No warehouse, configurable dashboards, advanced segmentation or alert service. [Eligibility tests](../crates/engine/tests/commercial_review.rs), [whole-original FX/export review](../crates/engine/tests/m9_integration_review.rs). |
| Product configuration and commercial documents — [Quoting](https://www.pricefx.com/platform/quoting/) describes templates, configuration and delivery | Partial | Flat products and CSV evidence export work. No configurable BOM, bundles, reusable quote templates, rendered customer documents, electronic signature or delivery. [Quote process/export checks](../scripts/e2e.py). |
| Promotions — [Promotions](https://www.pricefx.com/platform/promotions/) describes campaign calendars, combined offers, simulation and ROI analysis | Missing | No promotion plan/calendar, budget, stacking/eligibility model or effectiveness measurement. A negotiated discount is not a promotions workflow. |
| Channel management — [Channel Management](https://www.pricefx.com/platform/channel-management/) describes ship-and-debit claims, agreement validation, exceptions and payout support | Missing | No distributor/POS claim ingestion, claim validation, dispute handling or channel program. Invoice credits and rebate adjustments do not implement channel claims. |
| Optimization and AI — [AI Optimization](https://www.pricefx.com/platform/ai-optimization) describes list/deal recommendations using objectives, guardrails and business/market data; the [platform](https://www.pricefx.com/platform) describes monitoring agents | Missing | No optimizer, demand/elasticity model, win-rate model, recommendation model, agent or copilot. Rule calculations and synthetic scenarios are not AI or evidence of commercial benefit. |

Engine workflow checks are useful tests, not an enterprise security boundary. [Security scope](security.md). |
